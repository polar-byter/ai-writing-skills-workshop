# Lantern Keepers - Rulebook, page 4

## Taking a Turn

On your turn, move your Keeper up to two spaces. If you end your move on a Lighthouse space, draw one Storm card.

Storm cards are no piece of cake. Resolve the card right away, then put it face down on the discard pile.

If you run out of Oil tokens, your lantern goes dark and you skip your next turn. Don't sweat it: any other Keeper on your space can give you one Oil token.

Tip: keep an eye on the Storm deck. When the last card is drawn, the round ends.
